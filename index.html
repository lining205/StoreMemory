What this proves

Before learning, the topic agent ranks by views and picks entertainment. After the review agent writes its conclusion into shared memory, the same agent ranks by precise customer signals and picks owner-anxiety and preparation-checklist topics. The copy agent then drafts under stored brand rules: plain language, no diagnosis or outcome promises, and in-person checkup guidance.
